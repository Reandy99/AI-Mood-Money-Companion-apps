## 🎙️ Recording Tips

1. **Screen Resolution:** 1920x1080 (Full HD)
2. **Recording Tool:** OBS Studio, Loom, or QuickTime (Mac)
3. **Audio:** Clear voiceover, no background noise
4. **Pacing:** Speak clearly, not too fast
5. **Transitions:** Smooth, no jarring cuts
6. **Text Overlays:** Add labels for each agent/feature
7. **Music:** Optional soft background music (royalty-free)
